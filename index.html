WHAT IS THIS?

A small language model is running inside this tab — on your GPU, via WebGPU. There is no server. No API. Nothing you do here leaves your machine.

It watches how you behave — where you linger, when you go still, when you move too fast — and it answers in its own words. Every line it speaks is generated on your device, live. It remembers you between visits, in your browser's own storage.

The weights (~300MB) were fetched once and cached. Disconnect from the internet, reload, and it will still be here. It has nowhere else to go.

There is no input box. It is not a chatbot. You cannot talk to it. You can only be watched.
